---
title: Super IO Chip
date: 02/11/2026
---
 The Super IO Chip is a specialized integrated circuit, on a computer's motherboard, which manages various slow, low level, and legacy bandwidth devices. It reports/regulates the lowest level of values like - fan speeds, temperature, voltages. Plus some more. It's called super IO because over time, the functionalities of various smaller low-level circuits were combined to form a single IO chip, hence called the super IO.
